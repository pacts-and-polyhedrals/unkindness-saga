# Thorgrinn's Backstory — Canon Source

## The Ironfist Society
- The Government: A strict stratocracy ruled by a council of high-ranking generals rather than a king. Military rank dictates social class, resources and civic power.
- The Economy: A medieval mercenary and security corporation. The clan trades elite protection for tithes of gold, surface resources and raw materials from surrounding territories.
- The Methods: Harsh martial law, engineered roads and fortified mini-fortresses called Fingers.

## The Neverwinter Contract
The Ironfists operate as an Auxiliary Garrison in Neverwinter, patrolling dangerous subterranean routes and securing major surface roads.

## Thorgrinn & Baldrik — Raised in the Shield Wall
The brothers grew up in poverty in the lowest tiers of the hold. They entered training together, acting as a literal shield wall for one another and later serving as an inseparable patrol pair.

## The Fallout
Baldrik became consumed by a war deity and increasingly brutal. During a patrol he attempted to ritualistically execute desperate thieves; Thorgrinn physically intervened. Baldrik declared the family bond dead and volunteered for the classified Righteous Vanguard.

## Continuity correction
Thorgrinn remains in his mechanical Warforged-style body powered by a soul coin. He has not returned to his original organic dwarven form. His sword compulsion has been lifted and is not reactivated by this module.
