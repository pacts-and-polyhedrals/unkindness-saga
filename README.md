# The Unkindness Saga: Ravenloft Finale & Ironfist Legacy

One Foundry VTT module (v14 · dnd5e) for the Sunday group's whole run, from Wachter House to Kharad-Varr.

By **Asmo, Pacts & Polyhedrals (Ben Vale)** — [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/pactsandpolyhedrals) · StartPlaying: dmasmo

## Part I — The Cat and the Unkindness
The Curse of Strahd finale overlay, starting at the Nov 8 handoff beneath Wachter House: Arl's contract, Van Richten, Vallaki's cult war, Strahd's sealed letter, the brides (Volenta as the male Oathbreaker), Finn, Tatyana, three-phase Strahd, the War of Broken Gods (Orcus, Vampyr, Vecna), the Wish, the coffin chase and Barovia's collapse.
- **23 walled and lit scenes**: Castle Ravenloft grounds, every floor, spires, larders, dungeons and catacombs; Vallaki's war streets and arcane outfitter; the Last Road to the Gates of Ravenloft; Barovia unravelling into the mist.
- Portraits and tokens for the cast, objects and hazards; art on every item.
- The **Finale Dashboard** and 28 macros track the brides, Finn's chains, Strahd's phases, the Dark Power fronts, the Wish, the prophecies and Attention.
- World content lands in folders under *The Cat and the Unkindness*.

## Part II — The Shield Between Us: Ironfist Legacy
Thorgrinn comes home in a body of metal to Kharad-Varr. Eight chapters across twelve Sundays (29 Nov – 14 Feb).
- **15 painted battlemaps** with walls, doors, dark lighting, journal pins, tokens and music.
- **The Hold of Kharad-Varr**: a painted, linked overview of the whole mountain plus eight venue maps (the Low Lantern, the Gilded Nail, the Ember Baths, the Turning Gear, Anvilsong Forge, Glimmerhall, Mossbeard and Fernspit Remedies, the Hall of Deep Script), each with a keeper and a self-contained side quest tied back to the evidence and the war.
- 41 actors with dnd5e activities, evidence E1–E7, rune magic, original music, roll tables, card decks and the **Stone Ledger**.
- **Shadows Beyond Barovia** imports how Part I ended and weaves Orcus, Vampyr and Vecna through every session: Attention thresholds, signature rules, Dark Gifts with stated prices, Marks, combined arcs, outcome rules for each ending, reputation, prophecy boons and 38 Shadow beats with hooks.
- Content installs from compendium packs into *Ironfist Legacy* folders.

## Set up
Enable the module, reload as GM and choose **Set Up Everything** (or *Game Settings → The Unkindness Saga Manager*). The Manager can set up or repair each part separately. Player character sheets are never touched.

**Coming from the separate modules?** Disable *cat-and-unkindness* and *ironfist-legacy*, enable this one and reload. It migrates their world content, finale state and Stone Ledger automatically on first load, then *Set Up / Repair Part I* moves the Part I documents into their new folders and art paths without duplicating anything.

## Art on The Forge
If the host does not serve the module's own image and audio files (The Forge sometimes installs a custom module without them), the module loads them from its GitHub repository (`raw.githubusercontent.com/pacts-and-polyhedrals/unkindness-saga`) and rewrites the paths in the world documents it created. Keep the repository public and keep `module/assets` in it. Manager → **Repair Art** re-runs the check.
