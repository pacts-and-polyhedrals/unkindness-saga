# THE CAT AND THE UNKINDNESS
## Castle Ravenloft Finale for Strahd1

This module is the compact Foundry implementation of the two-session finale in which Castle Ravenloft becomes Strahd's instrument, his brides are the gauntlet, Finn is an emotional hinge rather than a disposable spawn, and Strahd progresses from amused narrator to frightened predator.

### Continuity locks
- The party is the **Unkindness**: Damien, Vesper, Bunch, Legna and Thorgrinn.
- Thorgrinn remains in his mechanical soul-coin body.
- Finn is a former companion, transformed and claimed through Strahd's Sanguine Moon influence, with a long-running possibility of salvation.
- The bride roster used here is **Anastrasya, Volenta and Ludmilla**, with Finn as Strahd's corrupted new addition. **Volenta is male and is an Oathbreaker paladin** in this Strahd1 continuity.
- Ireena/Tatyana is treated as a person with agency, not another combat objective.
- The Abbot survived earlier events and can be confronted socially at the wedding altar.
- The Unkindness has broken the Amber cycle that made Strahd's returns possible.
- Ordinary sunlight and *Daylight* do not defeat the Black Sun protection. Artifact-grade sunlight such as the Sunsword can.
- Baldrik and the Righteous Vanguard previously crossed Barovia under Vistani arrangements. Their unresolved black command wagon remains unresolved here.

### Finale rhythm
1. Last road and Vistani/Baldrik clue.
2. Azrathox delivers the party to Ravenloft while the castle isolates them.
3. Anastrasya and Volenta in the ballroom, with Volenta as the male Oathbreaker paladin and Anastrasya as the movement/manipulation partner.
4. Ludmilla in the Black Sun laboratory.
5. The Abbot at the wedding altar.
6. Finn and the three Chains of Claim.
7. Ireena/Tatyana chooses her own position.
8. Strahd Phase I — The Count Who Knows You.
9. Strahd Phase II — The Lord Is the Castle.
10. Strahd Phase III — The Devil Without the Mask.
11. Coffin chase and permanent death.
12. Collapse of Barovia and ejection onto an Ironfist road.
